Disposal Guidelines

Accepted vs. Prohibited Debris Materials

To ensure smooth disposal at local Michigan landfills, review our list of allowed household and construction materials.

Allowed Waste Materials

  • Household furniture, mattresses & appliances
  • Drywall, framing lumber, flooring & cabinetry
  • Roofing shingles, tile, siding & masonry
  • Yard waste, tree branches, sod & bushes
  • Concrete, brick & heavy stone (requires weight check)

Prohibited Materials

  • Liquid paint, solvents & toxic chemicals
  • Car batteries, motor oil & gasoline
  • Asbestos & biohazardous waste
  • Propane tanks & pressurized cylinders
  • Freon appliances (unless certified drained)
