Waste Material Guidelines

What Materials Can Go In Your Dumpster Rental?

We make waste disposal transparent and hassle-free. Review our list of accepted debris to ensure a smooth drop-off and pickup experience.

✔ Accepted Waste Materials

  • Household furniture, mattresses, couches, tables
  • Drywall, lumber, studs, flooring, carpet & tile
  • Roofing shingles, underlayment & flashing

✖ Prohibited Items

  • Liquid paint, solvents & toxic chemicals
  • Car batteries, motor oil & gasoline
