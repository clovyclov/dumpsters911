Job Site Waste Guidelines

What C&D Debris Can Go In A Commercial Dumpster?

Maintain an OSHA-compliant, safe construction job site. Review accepted commercial waste materials before scheduling drop-offs.

✔ Accepted Commercial Waste

  • Framing timber, studs, plywood & OSB
  • Drywall, sheetrock, ceiling tiles & insulation
  • Commercial roofing shingles, EPDM & metal decking
  • Concrete, brick, masonry & stone (heavy weight spec)
  • Commercial fixtures, office furniture & pallets

✖ Prohibited Items

  • Liquid paints, industrial solvents & adhesive drums
  • Car batteries, motor oil & fuel containers
  • Asbestos insulation & toxic materials
  • Propane tanks & pressurized canisters
  • Commercial refrigeration units (freon)
